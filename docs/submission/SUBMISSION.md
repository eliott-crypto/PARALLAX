# PARALLAX hackathon submission

## One-line pitch

PARALLAX gives early-stage investors a Claude research team to find overlooked startups and test what could break each thesis.

## Live product or output link

https://eliott-crypto.github.io/PARALLAX/

The hosted output lets judges explore actual recorded company research, citations and agent hand-offs. The full local product runs fresh Claude investigations with a server-side Anthropic key. The public output never exposes a key or pretends to run new research.

## Agent design

A discovery team combines two scouts, a price verifier, an adversarial analyst and a ranking committee. Bounded gap and identity checks resolve missing companies. Each company can trigger nine specialist Claude agents: Scout, Causal Mapper, Company Hunter, Founder Analyst, Competitive Analyst, Bull, Bear, Verifier and Committee. Founder and competitive research run in parallel, then Bull and Bear argue independently. Verifier searches fresh sources, Bull revises, and Committee decides. Typed outputs and a shared citation ledger carry evidence between agents. Durable storage preserves findings through failures. Token budgets, timeouts, cancellation and targeted recovery bound each attempt. The retained Browserbase case spans 36 minutes, including recovery and source review, and ends with all nine roles complete. Its verified customer-attribution correction changes the final thesis. A public replay exposes the actual outputs, events and usage.

## Five-minute table demo

**0:00–0:40. Customer and problem.** Emerging venture funds and angels need to decide which overlooked startups deserve scarce diligence time. Funding headlines and flattering company claims leave the hardest work to the analyst.

**0:40–1:35. The useful output.** Open a theme. Show the ranked shortlist, company logos, financing date and reported valuation. Open one company and show the brief connecting a world change to customer demand. End on its weakest assumption and next proof.

**1:35–2:25. The surprising interaction.** Open Universe and remove company claims. Show which companies drift away from the evidence core. Explain that distance measures cited support and height shows the agents’ confidence.

**2:25–3:40. The agents change their minds.** Open the recorded Browserbase case. Show the research team, Bear challenge, fresh verification and Bull revision. The concrete correction: Salesforce and Visa belonged to a competitor’s customer list. Show the final MONITOR decision and its unresolved commercial questions.

**3:40–4:25. Business.** Begin with emerging VC teams and angel syndicates. Test a subscription of $299 per analyst per month plus team plans. This is a pricing hypothesis. The pilot goal is five funds measuring useful leads, citation quality and diligence time saved.

**4:25–5:00. Trust and close.** Prices carry dates and evidence. Missing prices stay unranked. SAFE caps never masquerade as valuations. API keys remain server-side. Founder research covers public professional history. The next step is a paid pilot that tests whether better challenged shortlists improve investors’ research workflow.
